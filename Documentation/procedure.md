# How to Submit an Assignment

**Follow the steps below in order to submit an assignment!**

1. Open the course that you wish to submit the assignment in.
2. Go over to the gradebook tab.
3. Click on the desired assignment.
4. Then go to view instructions.
5. Make sure to read the instructions on what to submit.
6. Determine what file type is needed
7. Save Your document as that needed file type.
8. Click on "Upload a file"
9. This will automatically open your download folder in your system's File Explorer.
10. Select your desired document.
11. Click the "open" button.
12. Then click submit!
13. You will receive an automated message that you submitted your assignment!
14. Congrats you have successfully submitted your assignment!
